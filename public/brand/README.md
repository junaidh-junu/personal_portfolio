# Brand mark

The mark is the Arabic letter jim (ج), the first letter of Junaidh (جنيد), drawn as a single rounded stroke with its dot cradled inside the bowl. To readers of Arabic it is the letter; to everyone else it reads as a rounded J. The dot is the same blue dot the site uses for "available".

- mark-dark.svg: stroke #f4f4f1, dot #7c9cff. Use on dark backgrounds.
- mark-light.svg: stroke #141414, dot #2a56d8. Use on light backgrounds.
- tile.svg: the mark on a #0b0b0b rounded square. Favicon, app icon, avatar.
- wordmark-dark.svg, wordmark-light.svg: mark with "Junaidh Haneefa" in Geist Medium.
- icon-512.png: raster tile for stores and profiles.

Clear space: at least the dot's diameter on every side. Minimum size: 16px for the mark, 120px wide for the wordmark. Do not rotate, outline, recolour the stroke, or put the mark on a photo.
